NIMBUS LOGISTICS INC. (NASDAQ: NMBS)
Q2 2026 Earnings Conference Call
Date: May 14, 2026, 5:00 PM ET

PARTICIPANTS
Maria Chen — Chief Executive Officer
David Okafor — Chief Financial Officer
Operator
Analysts: Sarah Lin (Morgan Stanley), Tom Reyes (Baird)

-------------------------------------------------------------------

OPERATOR: Good afternoon, and welcome to Nimbus Logistics' Second Quarter 2026
Earnings Conference Call. I will now hand the call over to Maria Chen, CEO.

MARIA CHEN (CEO): Thank you, everyone. The second quarter played out in a
challenging and dynamic operating environment. Revenue came in at $495 million.
While we would have liked to see stronger top-line momentum, we believe the
underlying fundamentals of the business remain solid, and we are focused on the
levers within our control.

We are taking a disciplined approach to capital allocation in light of broader
macro uncertainty. We continue to make meaningful progress on operational
efficiency, and we are confident in the long-term opportunity ahead of us.

Our automation platform continues to be a strategic priority, and we are
investing thoughtfully to position the business for durable growth over time.
We remain committed to building a resilient, high-quality franchise.

DAVID OKAFOR (CFO): Thanks, Maria. Revenue of $495 million. Gross margin was 39%.
We are managing the cost structure prudently given the current environment. We
ended the quarter with $540 million in cash.

On guidance, we are updating our full-year revenue outlook to a range of $1.95
to $2.0 billion, reflecting a more measured view of the second half. We continue
to evaluate the optimal timeline for our capacity expansion initiatives to ensure
they align with demand.

OPERATOR: Our first question comes from Sarah Lin of Morgan Stanley.

SARAH LIN: Last quarter you committed to adjusted EBITDA profitability by Q4.
Is that still the plan?

MARIA CHEN: Sarah, what I would say is that we are intensely focused on the path
to profitability, and we believe we have the right strategy. In the current
environment, we think it is prudent to prioritize sustainable, high-quality
growth rather than anchoring to a specific near-term timeframe. The fundamentals
support our confidence in the trajectory.

SARAH LIN: And the West Coast hub — is that still on track for Q3?

DAVID OKAFOR: We are continuing to evaluate the optimal phasing of that project.
We want to be thoughtful about aligning capacity with the demand environment.
We will share more as plans firm up.

TOM REYES (Baird): Can you give us the enterprise customer count and net revenue
retention this quarter?

DAVID OKAFOR: We are increasingly focused on the quality and strategic value of
our customer relationships rather than headline counts. We are pleased with the
engagement we are seeing across our enterprise base.

MARIA CHEN: In closing, while the environment is dynamic, we remain confident in
our strategy and the resilience of our franchise. Thank you all.
