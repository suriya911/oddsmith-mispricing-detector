Q1 2026 Earnings Conference Call
Date: February 12, 2026, 5:00 PM ET

PARTICIPANTS
Maria Chen — Chief Executive Officer

OPERATOR: Good afternoon, and welcome to Nimbus Logistics' First Quarter 2026
Earnings Conference Call. I will now hand the call over to Maria Chen, CEO.

MARIA CHEN (CEO): Thank you, and good afternoon everyone. I am pleased to report
a very strong first quarter. Revenue grew 22% year-over-year to $480 million,
well ahead of our guidance. Our automated fulfillment platform is performing
exceptionally, and customer adoption is accelerating.

I want to be direct about our priorities for the year. First, we are committed
to reaching adjusted EBITDA profitability by Q4 2026 — this is a firm target and
we have a clear path to it. Second, we expect our new West Coast automation hub
to be fully operational by the end of Q3, adding 30% to our processing capacity.
Third, we are guiding to full-year revenue of $2.1 billion, representing 20%+
growth.

Our enterprise customer count reached 1,240, up 18% from last quarter, and net
revenue retention stands at a healthy 118%. These are the metrics that define our
business, and they are all moving in the right direction.

DAVID OKAFOR (CFO): Thank you, Maria. Let me walk through the numbers. Revenue
of $480 million, up 22%. Gross margin expanded to 41%, up from 38% a year ago.
Operating expenses were well controlled. We ended the quarter with $610 million
in cash and a clear line of sight to our Q4 profitability target.

We are reaffirming full-year guidance of $2.1 billion in revenue and adjusted
EBITDA breakeven exiting the year. Our West Coast hub remains on schedule for
Q3 completion.

OPERATOR: Our first question comes from Sarah Lin of Morgan Stanley.

SARAH LIN: Congratulations on the quarter. Can you talk about the path to the
Q4 profitability target — how confident are you?

MARIA CHEN: Very confident, Sarah. The unit economics are working. As volume
flows through the automation platform, incremental margins are strong. We see
a clear path and we are committed to that Q4 target.

TOM REYES (Baird): On the West Coast hub — any risk to the Q3 timeline?

DAVID OKAFOR: No, the build is on track. We expect it operational by end of Q3
as planned. This is a key driver of our second-half capacity story.

MARIA CHEN: In closing, this was a strong quarter and we are executing well
against a clear plan. Thank you all.
